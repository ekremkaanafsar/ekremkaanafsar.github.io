The question

Wildfires are an environmental outcome, but they are also political. This project asks how municipal partisanship and landed elites relate to wildfire outcomes across Turkey's municipalities.

Building the panel

The heart of the project is a dataset built end to end from messy raw sources: a municipality-year panel of about 1,389 Turkish municipalities from 2002 to 2025.

  • Fires from space. NASA FIRMS VIIRS satellite fire detections, clustered in space and time with DBSCAN.
  • Landed elites. A Landed Elite Index built with principal components analysis (PCA).
  • Environmental controls. ERA5 reanalysis and ESA Copernicus data.

Methods

The analysis uses count and hurdle models and fixed-effects specifications. A regression discontinuity design is planned as the next step. I run all of the statistical analyses in Stata and R.
