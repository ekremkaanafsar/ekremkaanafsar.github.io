About

Hi, I'm Kaan.

My way into energy ran through the Twin Tiers, the New York-Pennsylvania border region where wind farms and fracking grew side by side. Studying it with Prof. Robert A. Holahan at Binghamton University showed me how much markets and regulation shape what actually gets built. Energy markets and the energy transition are the field I chose, and I want my quantitative work to shape real investment and policy decisions.

I enjoy the whole life of a dataset: collecting it, cleaning it, checking it. And I find it fascinating to turn the results into charts and visuals, making the complex understandable, and making boring fascinating.

I also enjoy teaching and explaining. In spring 2026 I led weekly Python labs for more than 40 undergraduates, from data wrangling to a first taste of machine learning.

I studied in a joint dual-diploma program between Middle East Technical University (METU) in Ankara and Binghamton University, and I'm bilingual in Turkish and English.
